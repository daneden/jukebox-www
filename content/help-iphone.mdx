<Faq q="Why does Playback ask for access to my music?">
Playback reads your Apple Music library **on your device** to build queues from
the songs and playlists you already have. It needs the Media & Apple Music
permission to do this. Grant it under **Settings → Playback → Media & Apple
Music**, or when the app first asks.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I control Playback with Siri?">
Yes. Ask Siri to make a playlist, design one by mood, play something at random,
or save what&rsquo;s playing to your library. You can also add a
**Control Center** button to start the music without unlocking your phone. It
all runs on **Shortcuts**, so you can wire Playback into routines of your own.
</Faq>
