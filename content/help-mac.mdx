<Faq q="Why does Playback ask for access to my music?">
  Playback reads your Apple Music library **on your Mac** to build queues from
  the songs and playlists you already have, and controls playback via the Music
  app. macOS will prompt you the first time; you can change it later under
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music**.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I control Playback with Siri and Shortcuts?">
  Yes. Ask Siri to make a playlist, design one by mood, play something at
  random, or save what&rsquo;s playing to your library. Playback&rsquo;s actions
  are available in the **Shortcuts** app on macOS, so you can build them into
  your own automations and menu-bar workflows.
</Faq>
