<Faq q="Do I need an Apple Music subscription?">
  No. Playback works with any music added to your Apple Music library, either
  through an Apple Music subscription or imported from elsewhere.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Playback collect any of my data?">
  No. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Your library stays on
  your device, between you and Apple Music. See the [Privacy
  Policy](/privacy-policy) for the full details.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How does Playback choose what to play?">
  In **Songs**, Playback draws an endless rotation from your own library. A
  bespoke algorithm blends genre lineage, tempo, and sonic similarity,
  prioritising the songs you&rsquo;ve played most and those that have been in
  your library longest. The result is a queue that feels curated from music you
  already care about.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I get back to a queue I loved?">
  Yes. Every played queue is saved to Playback&rsquo;s **History**, so you can
  revisit a session and save it as a playlist in Apple Music.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How do the random playlists work?">
  **Playlists** spins a dial through your Apple Music playlists and lands on one
  at random &mdash; your collection reshuffled, with none of the deciding.
</Faq>

<Faq q="What is Design mode?">
  **Design** lets you build a playlist by drawing its shape. Sketch an energy
  curve &mdash; from glacial calm up to full intensity &mdash; and Playback
  fills it with songs that rise and fall to match your mood.
</Faq>
